## Why this feedback is preserved

The expert did not reject the hypothesis on its scientific merits. He reported that he could not evaluate it because the starting point, the proposed stages of zero, and the key term “spatial fixedness” were not sufficiently clear at the beginning of the document. This is recorded as a communication failure that must be corrected before substantive evaluation can occur.

The main lesson is methodological and generalizable beyond this project:

1. Establish the starting point before introducing the proposed mechanism.
2. Define nonstandard terms before relying on them.
3. Explain whether a staged model is established, adapted, or newly proposed.
4. Connect every borrowed idea to prior work with appropriate citations.
5. Make the abstract self-contained enough that a specialist can understand the research question without reading forward to recover missing definitions.
