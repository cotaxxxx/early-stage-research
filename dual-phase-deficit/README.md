## Scope

This project studies a phase-dependent variance functional for planar convex bodies using support and radial functions, with a fully worked rectangle-family model. The manuscript presents the rectangle results as proved within the draft and separates them from broader conjectures for general convex-body families and higher dimensions.

## Main claims in the manuscript

- zero-set characterization by rotational-similarity self-polarity;
- exact second-order curvature at the symmetric phase and a critical rectangle aspect ratio;
- fourth-order degeneracy at the critical value and a square-root local bifurcation law;
- existence, uniqueness, critical absorption, and strict monotonicity of the global minimizing phase branch for rectangles;
- broader conjectures for general convex bodies and higher-dimensional rotation groups.
